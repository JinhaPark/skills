# Release checks

Inspect original evidence and calculations independently of the main author where supported. Preserve actual review traces and findings; self-review is not independent multi-agent verification.

Verify industry growth versus profit capture, predetermined pool and later membership changes, selection/data-access bias, whole-pool NTM FCF/CDS comparability and NA, screening-to-valuation reasoning, two-to-three-year reinvestment/cash conversion, prior publication exclusion, and valid no_selection/blocked branches.

Recheck price/date/currency/share classes/ADR ratio, market-cap shares versus EPS/valuation diluted shares, TTM overlap, segment totals, revenue/margin/profit links, EPS, CFO/investment/FCF bridge, growth/reinvestment/ROIC, discount timing/terminal value, net debt/leases, tax/SBC double counting, p/horizon/equity recovery/P-Q/risk duplication, peer suitability, targets/total return, sources, scenario labels and conclusions.

Opening table, cover and body must use the same accepted data/model versions. Every substantive section must have explanation, evidence and counterarguments. Resolve critical findings and rerun affected checks; do not mark review passed on the strength of a worker's completion claim alone.

Never backfill plausible core numbers. A material unresolved gap lowers confidence or blocks publication; explain minor limitations and continue useful work. Missing distress-only inputs permit base DCF plus mandatory NA comparison, not invented adjustment. Save reproducible formulas/executable model and validation provenance. Gate-script success certifies record integrity only, not truth or actual delegation.

Verify one complete report.md is durably available through the supported delivery channel. Keep full models, handoffs and reviews as internal checkpoints. Record completed-stock coverage only after actual delivery; distinguish no_selection, blocked and interrupted publication. Never claim file storage, external delivery or completed reviews without evidence.
