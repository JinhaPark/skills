# Publication outcomes and thesis assessment

## Preserve the original judgment

Create an immutable `recommendation.json` alongside the final pre-delivery report, using `assets/recommendation-template.json`. Hash both into the publication manifest. Complete the research judgment before observing future prices. Record a separate delivery receipt with the actual publication timestamp/reference; never rewrite the frozen snapshot to add later facts. Assemble a derived assessment input from the snapshot, receipt and dated observation events, retaining all input hashes.

Freeze the entity/share class, decision (buy/watch/avoid/no_selection), reference price and currency, entry convention, original target horizon, scenario ranges and business drivers, expected returns and their dividend/FX/tax convention, benchmark, and measurable thesis/falsification criteria. Set each criterion's metric definition, unit, accounting period, threshold, operator and source. Include a narrative thesis and an explicit record of unavailable fields. A new thesis or horizon is a dated revision; it does not replace the old evaluation endpoint.

Use the original valuation reference price to explain the published recommendation. If it predates publication, label performance as a reference-price comparison, not a tradable return. An executable-signal study must freeze the entry rule before the next tradable price is known, append that entry observation later, and keep it separate from the published reference-price study. Never imply the user actually traded.

For `publication_reference`, store the original price in `entry` and require its timestamp no later than publication. For `next_executable_close`, freeze `entry_rule=first_eligible_session_close`, retain the original valuation price in `reference_entry`, and leave `entry=null` until observed. Append an `entry_event` with `entry` plus `entry_session={for_publication_at,close_at,source_refs}`; verify independently that the cited exchange calendar identifies the first eligible close, and require the entry timestamp to match it exactly. Missing session evidence means an unavailable executable baseline, not permission to choose a later entry. Use full price-record fields for both baseline types. Record unavailable fields as objects such as `{"field":"entry","reason":"first eligible close has not occurred"}`; do not accept empty placeholders as completed research.

## Preserve the published cover letter

During final assembly, save the exact four published cover paragraphs in `cover.md` and include those paragraphs verbatim in `report.md`. Freeze `cover_letter={path:"cover.md",sha256,summary}` in `recommendation.json`. Compute SHA-256 from actual cover bytes. Require nonempty cover text, four paragraphs, and the trimmed cover text as a substring of the final report (line-ending normalization is allowed; substantive wording changes are not). Register `cover.md` among the independent prepublication reflection's hashed reviewed artifacts alongside the report and recommendation.

Write `cover_letter.summary` as one concise paragraph of at most 50 whitespace-delimited words faithfully summarizing the published cover letter. Do not introduce a new investment thesis during dashboard editing. The public weekly paragraph uses this summary as its investment logic, with `thesis_source=published_cover_letter` and `cover_sha256` equal to the frozen cover hash. The reviewer checks semantic fidelity; a hash proves identity, not the quality of a summary. No-selection reports also preserve their four-paragraph cover and summary. Historical snapshots without these fields remain unchanged; show an explicit original-cover-unavailable reason instead of inventing one.

## Freeze dashboard entry and exit terms

Freeze `target_entry`, `take_profit` and `stop_loss` in every new recommendation, each as `{"value":null,"currency":null,"na_reason":"Not specified in the original recommendation."}` or a positive finite price with its currency and `na_reason=null`. These are the original target entry, TP and SL, not the observed baseline or a claim that a trade occurred. Never infer a stop from a downside scenario or turn a valuation range into an execution instruction. For no-selection records explicitly mark these terms N/A with the reason.

Numeric TP/SL also require frozen `barrier_basis={currency,price_basis:"split_adjusted_ex_dividend",share_basis}` and currencies matching that basis. Record the actual original per-share basis; never compare a total-return index or post-split unadjusted price directly with an old target. Subsequent corporate-action adjustments belong in sourced derived monitoring observations, not rewritten original terms. When both TP and SL are N/A, an incomplete barrier basis is allowed and must not imply price-event monitoring is possible.

Use the existing `target_horizon` for the third exit condition, horizon expiry. Keep its original `end_date`, exchange timezone and close rule. Optional `duration` and `start_basis` preserve the originally stated interval and anchor. If an endpoint was not specified, keep `end_date=null` and a nonempty `reason`; do not invent a standard three-, six- or twelve-month horizon. A duration awaiting an entry anchor does not establish an expiry timestamp. The helper returns `horizon_unavailable` with no mature return when the endpoint is absent, while still evaluating available original thesis criteria.

Preserve original snapshot bytes. For older reports lacking these newly introduced fields, derive dashboard N/A values with the reason "Not recorded in the original report" without modifying their snapshots or fabricating a target. Restore an original documented value only from its original source, preserving provenance and a dated correction record. New release gates require explicit target/NA fields; older assessment inputs with a recorded horizon remain compatible.

TP, SL and expiry are three separately visible conditions. Check observed events each scheduled run using sourced observations and the original entry/activation convention. Do not claim intraday/realtime monitoring or automatic order execution. Keep a TP/SL touch, the evaluation triggered by it, and the original-horizon return as separate dated records. If both prices were touched in the same bar and their ordering cannot be established, record the ambiguity rather than choosing a profitable first event. Retain thesis evaluation and missing-data reasons in each event assessment. Barrier monitoring does not overwrite the original forecast or stop the separate original-horizon study.

## Include the full cohort

Restore all confirmed publications, including losses, delistings, acquired companies, corrections and withdrawn theses. Deduplicate by report ID; track revised recommendations as distinct dated vintages linked to the original. Preserve unresolved/missing entries in denominators and disclose coverage. Evaluate buy recommendations as their own cohort; watch, avoid, and no_selection are process decisions, not automatically long or short positions. Do not assume continuous portfolio reinvestment from a weekly series of isolated ideas.

HPQ's user-confirmed 2026-10-24 publication remains in history. If its actual report, target horizon or baseline cannot be recovered, retain the company exclusion and mark performance `legacy_unavailable`; do not invent the missing original forecast. Missing data are not zero returns or wins. A mature subset and an interim subset must never be silently pooled.

Persist a normalized local ledger snapshot before assessment: `{"publications":[{"publication_key":"...","report_id":null,"status":"delivered","provenance":"user_provided"}],"coverage_complete":false,"coverage_gap_reason":"..."}`. Use the actual report ID when known; otherwise construct a stable history key such as `legacy:HP-Inc:2026-10-24`, explicitly a synthetic history key rather than a report identifier. Use `provenance=delivery_receipt` for observed delivery records and `user_provided` for the HPQ seed. Preserve entity, date and original persistent source references as additional fields. Never silently promote an unavailable external ledger to complete coverage.

Use this exact outer structure for `assessment/track-record.json`: `{"run_id":"...","as_of":"timezone-aware timestamp","ledger_reference":"relative/path/to/restored-ledger.json","ledger_sha256":"actual file SHA-256","coverage_complete":false,"coverage_gap_reason":"...","rows":[{"publication_key":"...","report_id":null,"status":"legacy_unavailable"}]}`. Every confirmed ledger publication key must occur exactly once, with the same actual report ID or null; no extra or omitted keys. Each row additionally records its applicable original horizon, recommendation, source/snapshot/helper-artifact references and hashes, returns, thesis conclusions and limitations. Both coverage fields must agree with the restored snapshot; false requires a nonempty explanation. Row reconciliation proves coverage of that known snapshot only. If the external ledger is missing, restore the known subset including the seed, disclose the gap, and never claim all historical publications were independently recovered.

## Respect time and comparison basis

Assess at the original horizon close. For a nontrading horizon date use the first tradable session on/after it under the predeclared exchange-calendar rule and cite the calendar. Record actual endpoint date and the gap. Preserve the maturity result on future runs; new events can add a revision/correction, not substitute a later favorable price. Before maturity, show interim observations and thesis monitoring, explicitly pending the final horizon result. An interim high touching a target is distinct from the horizon-close result.

Use the same share basis across entry, endpoint and cash distributions. Reconcile splits, ADR changes, spin-offs, mergers, cash proceeds, bankruptcy recovery and delisting using sourced event records. Carry terminal proceeds through the original horizon according to the frozen cash/reinvestment convention; do not discard a failed entity or replace it with the surviving ticker. If this bridge cannot be verified, keep the return unavailable and describe the event. A confirmed total loss may be zero terminal proceeds; lack of a quote alone is not proof of zero recovery.

Separate local-currency price change, base-currency price change, and total return. For cash dividends without reinvestment, `TR = (endpoint price × endpoint FX + cash distributions in base currency per entry share) / (entry price × entry FX) − 1`. Convert distributions at their actual receipt-date FX; endpoint FX is not a substitute. Distinguish tax assumptions. A total-return index already incorporates its stated distribution/reinvestment policy, so do not add dividends again. Adjusted-price vendor fields require a documented definition; “adjusted” alone is insufficient. Zero dividends also need verification.

Freeze an economically relevant industry benchmark and, when useful, a broad-market benchmark at publication. Use matching holding dates, base currency and dividend/reinvestment conventions for excess returns. Do not change benchmarks after seeing outcomes. If only a retrospectively chosen benchmark is available, show it as supplemental and exclude it from the predeclared excess-return cohort. Timing differences between markets must be disclosed. Report sample counts and data completeness; small, overlapping, regime-specific samples cannot establish skill or causation.

## Evaluate thesis independently of price

For each original thesis/scenario, compare actual business drivers, revenue, margins, reinvestment, cash conversion, leverage, dilution and catalysts with the original dated forecast. Use the accounting period originally forecast; do not replace it with an easier period. Separate the first available release from later restatements and record which observation is used. Mark unavailable periods pending. Reconcile price appreciation with the operating evidence: an appreciated stock can have a falsified thesis, and a valid operating thesis can have a negative return.

Classify narrative thesis as supported, weakened, falsified, mixed or not yet evaluable, with claim-level source references and the independent assessor's reasons. A numerical trigger is evidence, not automatic proof that every part of a thesis failed. Report forecast error only for compatible definitions/periods; scenario-band inclusion is descriptive, not a probability-calibration score when no probabilities were originally stated. Preserve catalyst timing misses and alternative explanations. Attribute execution/data/method errors separately from unpredictable events; do not infer a workflow defect from an isolated loss.

## Deterministic helper contract

Run `python scripts/assess_recommendation.py recommendation.json OBSERVATIONS.json --receipt delivery-receipt.json --as-of TIMEZONE_AWARE_TIMESTAMP`. Pass the original immutable recommendation bytes, not a rewritten post-delivery snapshot. The receipt requires `report_id`, `status=delivered`, `recommendation_sha256` matching those bytes, `report_sha256` matching the snapshot's `frozen_report_sha256`, `publication_at` and actual `report_reference`. The helper derives the delivered input in memory, records snapshot/receipt/observation hashes, and never changes either original file. It also merges an optional sourced `entry_event` only into a previously empty executable-entry slot. A legacy record with `original_snapshot_available=false` can be passed without a receipt only as an explicitly user-confirmed delivered ledger record; it produces no performance numbers.

The helper returns JSON on stdout. It checks arithmetic, temporal fields, compatible share/FX/dividend bases and supplied numerical thesis triggers. It does not fetch evidence, verify a calendar source, classify narrative thesis, certify external delivery or save data. Preserve input/output hashes and independently inspect sources; hash linkage proves consistency of supplied records, not the truth of their external claims.

- Derived INPUT for the pure `assess` function: `report_id`, `publication_status=delivered`, `original_snapshot_available`, actual `publication_at`, `recommendation`, `base_currency`, `entry_convention=publication_reference|next_executable_close`, `entry`, `target_horizon.end_date/exchange_timezone/market_close_rule=first_session_on_or_after`, optional `benchmark.entry`, and `thesis_criteria`. An executable entry also requires the frozen `entry_rule` and sourced `entry_session` above. The CLI constructs these publication fields through the hash-checked receipt join.
- A price/index record: `value`, timezone-aware `observed_at` (exchange-local date), `currency`, `basis=split_adjusted_ex_dividend|total_return_index`, common `share_basis`, `fx_to_base`, `source_refs`; foreign currency also needs `fx_source_refs`. A verified zero endpoint needs `kind=terminal_proceeds` and an event bridge in supporting evidence.
- OBSERVATIONS: optional `endpoint`, `dividends`, `benchmark_endpoint`, `benchmark_dividends`, `entry_event`, and `thesis` keyed by original criterion ID. A mature outcome always requires `horizon_session={for_date,close_at,source_refs}` mapping the original horizon to the exact first eligible exchange close, even when the horizon is itself a trading day. Require the endpoint timestamp to equal that close. Intraday quotes are interim; before the evidenced close the horizon is not mature. Missing session evidence leaves mature performance unavailable. Resolve dates in the frozen `exchange_timezone`.
- Cash distributions: `base_cash_per_entry_share`, `base_currency`, `share_basis`, exact `period_start/period_end` matching the price observations, and `source_refs`. Exclude pre-entry or post-endpoint cash. Omit when unknown; never insert zero for convenience. Omit for total-return indices.
- Numerical criteria: `id`, `metric_id`, `period_end`, `unit`, `currency`, `trigger_operator` from `< <= > >= ==`, and `trigger_threshold`. Observations repeat the definition/period and add `value`, `available_at`, `source_refs`. Triggers represent original falsification conditions, not success thresholds.
- Outputs distinguish `matured`, `interim`, `pending_horizon`, `awaiting_horizon_data`, `baseline_unavailable`, `horizon_unavailable`, `legacy_unavailable` and `not_a_stock_recommendation`. Missing inputs stay unavailable; invalid/future/mismatched inputs raise errors. `price_return` uses the base currency; `local_price_return` isolates local prices. A total-return index cannot supply price-only appreciation. Mismatched dividend conventions suppress excess return until reconciled.

Record a separate sourced scenario/thesis narrative and cohort reconciliation around this helper. Do not promote a helper's `matured` status into independent confirmation of the inputs. Preserve every original result and each later assessment as append-only dated records.
