# Report structure and comparison policy

Write natural Korean honorific prose for an educated personal investor. Avoid translation-like language, unnecessary English, inflated rhetoric and false decimal precision. Explain how facts affect profits, cash and value; tables never replace the argument.

Use a selected-company title '기업명(티커) — 한 문장 투자 판단'; otherwise '산업명 — 이번 주 선정 없음'. Deliver one report.md, not separate final reports. Supporting artifacts remain internal checkpoints.

## Opening comparison

Immediately after title and price/data cutoff, show the entire previously approved peer pool, before the four cover paragraphs. Mark the selected company if any. The table is evidence for selection, not a peer group invented after picking a favorite. Keep previously covered and CDS-unavailable peers when economically appropriate; explain pool limits rather than filling a quota.

Required fields: company/ticker, expected next-12-month normalized FCF, current market cap, NTM FCF yield (%), actual CDS premium (bp, preferably comparable five-year contracts), forecast/observation date and sources. For mobile readability move amount/market-cap support into adjacent notes if necessary, but keep FCF yield and CDS in the same table.

NTM is the actual 12-month interval following the price cutoff, with explicit start/end dates. Never substitute historical TTM or the whole next fiscal year. If calendarizing fiscal forecasts, explain seasonality, weighting and limitations. Label each company's guidance/verified consensus/own estimate; align definitions and periods.

Calculate screening FCF yield as comparable normalized equity-basis FCF divided by current contemporaneous market cap. Document interest, taxes, leases, SBC, capex and net borrowing treatment; reconcile company differences. Do not divide FCFF by market cap; show FCFF/enterprise value separately if useful. Do not treat debt proceeds as recurring operating cash creation. Numerator/denominator for each firm use the same currency; disclose presentation currency/FX/share basis. Negative FCF yields stay negative. Unavailable inputs stay NA with reasons.

Use verified actual CDS observations only. Verify entity, tenor, currency, seniority, restructuring terms, quote type, date and liquidity. Align terms/timing and flag differences. Distinguish standard coupon, upfront payment, par spread and converted spread; a fixed standard coupon is not the market premium. Never fill the CDS column with ratings, bond spreads or invented estimates.

Below the table briefly explain industry growth/profit capture, peer pricing/credit differences, and why comparison supports candidate narrowing and selection/no_selection. Test whether high yield reflects transient cash, leverage or decline. Never subtract CDS from FCF yield or interpret their difference as riskless excess return. This is the core candidate-screening comparison; absolute DCF and mandatory distress comparison remain required for final valuation.

## Four cover paragraphs

Write the cover last from the reviewed body; place it immediately after the opening table/necessary notes. Exactly four short substantive paragraphs:
1. Industry growth, who captures profit, and this week's conclusion.
2. Peer FCF/CDS differences and candidate/rejection reasoning.
3. Selected firm's cash conversion, Damodaran value and current price.
4. Judgment, approach, main risks and observable falsification conditions.

For no_selection, paragraph 3 explains which price/value/evidence criteria were not met and paragraph 4 gives reopening conditions. Do not imply a completed company valuation where none was executed.

## Deep report

Order: industry demand/supply/profit capture → peer pool/comparison/candidate narrowing → selected business/competition → historical results/forecasts → normalized FCF/capital allocation → going-concern DCF/mandatory distress comparison → multiple/reverse-DCF checks → business scenarios/conditional returns → catalysts/risks/falsification → sources/assumptions/verification provenance.

For no_selection retain comparison, industry thesis, rejection grounds and revisit conditions; shorten irrelevant company-model sections honestly. For blocked critical evidence disclose blockage rather than calling every company unattractive.

Authors deliver complete paragraphs, required tables, linked evidence, assumptions and counterarguments for their assigned sections. Use summaries only for handoffs. Preserve useful depth during integration; remove duplicate explanations, not unique evidence. Keep common metric definitions, units, scenarios and conclusions consistent across all sections. Show important limits in the report; store verbose process logs separately.
